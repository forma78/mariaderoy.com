Questions

Who is the audit for?

Small business owners, founders, creators and solopreneurs, with a business or personal brand.

I’m just starting. Is it too early?

No. An audit at the start helps you choose your niche and position before you invest in visuals.

What industries do you work with?

Beauty, FMCG, consumer goods, HoReCa, B2C, luxury, tech and IT, and the principles apply to any category.

What happens after I book?

You’ll receive a link to choose a time slot for our Zoom call.

How does the audit work?

We start with a Zoom call and go through your brand together. After the call, you receive a PDF deck with the findings and prioritised recommendations, plus 30 days of chat support for any questions.
